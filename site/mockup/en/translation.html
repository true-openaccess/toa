Translation status

English is TOA's sole normative language for text and terminology. Other languages track English and fall back to it where untranslated.

1Progress

Generated at build time by scripts/i18n/status.py. Entries are stale if English changed after translation.

2Publication criteria

  • New languages start as incubating: built for preview only, hidden from menus.
  • A language is published once navigation, home, and agent pages reach 100%, other pages reach ≥80%, and a reviewer approves.
  • Pages under 80% fall back to English; 80%–99% show English for missing strings. Both display an English notice.
